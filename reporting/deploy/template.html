Why Lockeroom Testing Matters

Our Physicals give you real data to guide your training and proven paths for longevity.

VO2 Max: #1 Predictor of Longevity

Measures how efficiently your heart, lungs, and muscles use oxygen.

Why it matters: Higher VO2 Max means longer life and lower disease risk.

Research: 4–5× lower risk of death with higher VO2 (JAMA Cardiology, 2018).

Real-world: Walk further without stopping, recover faster, keep pace on family bike rides.

Strength: Your Lifelong Insurance Policy

Your ability to move, lift weight and control your body.

Why it matters: Strength protects independence, movement quality, and bone density.

Research: Stronger people live longer (Saeterbakken et al., 2022).

Real-world: Carry the groceries in one trip, lift your kids with ease.

Power: Your Reaction & Safety Buffer

Your ability to move fast and react quickly.

Why it matters: Power helps prevent falls and injuries.

Research: Leg power predicts falls better than strength (Skelton et al., 2002).

Real-world: Step off a curb quickly, catch yourself before falling, sprint for your flight.

The Bottom Line: We test what matters so you can live a longer, stronger life.
